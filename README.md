## Hi there 👋

Hi I'm Twiggy, AKA Jazz. This github will be a repository of my efforts to document my homelabs and learning experience for cybersecurity. I hope this will demonstrate my skills and passion for the world of cyber.



<details>
  <summary>Career So Far</summary>
    <p>I started my career in cyber a little later than most. My first professional experience was with music as a violin teacher and sound engineer for my local concert venue. I've taught many individuals and groups from Pre-K to adults in the fun but challenging art of classical music. My specialty was the Baroque time period. </p>
  <p>After a few years of teaching, I started my career in the corporate world with CaptionCall, assisting the elderly and hard of hearing community by providing real time captions for their phone calls. As fulfilling as this path was, I had an itch I just couldn't scratch after learning some simple scripting with Javascript and watching the amazing show Mr. Robot. </p>
  <p>
    I had my first dip into the enterprise world of cyber with Tek-Experts, a contractor for Microsoft. I learned cloud computing and networking while supporting our clients with Exchange Online and Outlook. The different nuances between a regular Enterprise tenant and a Government Cloud Community (GCC) tenant both surprised and intrigued me. I had no idea how complex and intertwined these different systems could be. My own lack of understanding on how even basic things like networks worked led me to study the CompTIA Network+ certification.
  </p>
<p>
  While supporting emails was fun, I found myself drawn more and more to network systems and ended up landing a job as a NOC Network Engineer with Comcast. Here I truly learned how routing, firewalls and switches really worked. I quickly found myself drowning in documentation from Cisco, Fortinet and Meraki, absorping as much information as I could. I felt like I thrived when I was fixing errant firewall rules, mismatched vpn configurations, or even doing something as simple as identifying a device was connected to the wrong vlan. Watching a packet capture record broadcast storms that turned back into regular two-way traffic was extremely fascinating to me. 
</p>
<p>
  I took so much pride into this work that my employer even recognized me several times for my efforts. I have been awarded the People's Champion award twice, and the Customer EXCEL award for my dedication in assisting my teammates with the tips and tricks I've learned over the years as well as assisting our high profile (ie Fortune 500) clients from start to finish on highly complex networking issues.
</p>
<p>
  Now I feel like my career could use a little upgrade. I am currently studying cybersecurity by following the Hack the Box CPST (Penetration Testing) job path. I am currently supplementing this with Python and Bash learning, and I am determined to learn every aspect of Linux that I can with the Linux Bible.
</p>
<p>
  Please feel free to keep up with my journey as I level up my skills and reach for my cyber goals.
</p>
